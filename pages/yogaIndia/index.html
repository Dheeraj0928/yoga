Yoga Goals India : Yoga Training School

Yoga has been in existence from time immemorial and transformed the lives of millions of people who sought to find their inner light, connect with the divine and begin their journey of self-discovery. In modern times, Yoga has assumed a great significance as the daily hustle and bustle threatens to overpower our senses and disconnect us from our inner reality and steer us towards restlessness and misery. Yoga is the beginning of the illuminated path your soul could traverse as it navigates its way towards the ultimate truth. The right foundation for your spiritual journey could ensure the journey is free of obstacles and is joyful.

Yoga Teacher Training in Rishikesh, India

Yoga Teacher Training Courses (YTTC) offer a comprehensive approach to learning yoga since they lay the groundwork for anyone who desires to understand yoga and its practices while also advancing their own practices. It is not just intended for people who want to teach yoga. Yoga TTC is best suited for anyone who wants to learn about yoga in-depth, practically, and holistically, regardless of whether they desire to become yoga teachers. Yoga Alliance requires students who want to become recognized yoga teachers around the world to complete a minimum of 200 hours of training.

YOGA ALLIANCE CERTIFICATION

Certified Yoga School in Rishikesh | Goa | Nepal

Yoga Goals India has received certification from the Yoga Alliance USA as a Registered Yoga School (RYS) (RYS200, RYS300, RYS500), a Registered Children Yoga School (RCYS), and a Yoga Alliance Continuing Education Provider (YACEP) (USA).
For those who are already certified as Registered Yoga Teachers (RYT) with Yoga Alliance USA, our yoga courses can be used to fulfill their Continuing Education (CE) training requirements.

The Yoga Goals would like to encourage each and every student to enroll in the course with the intention of developing their individual practices rather than only focusing on earning a certificate. However, if you want to share your yoga knowledge and expertise in the modern era, a certificate is significant and useful.
